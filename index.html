Our mission

Extending survival through cutting-edge, evidence-based therapies.

The GLORY Team is a gynecologic oncology research group at Yonsei University College of Medicine. From diagnosis and surgery to treatment and follow-up, we connect patient care with investigator-initiated trials, international clinical studies and translational research.
