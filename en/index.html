Mission

Extending survival through
cutting-edge, evidence-based therapies

The GLORY Team advances patient care in gynecologic cancer through innovative, research-driven therapies. Clinical research and patient care move together across the full continuum — from diagnosis and surgery to systemic treatment and long-term follow-up.

Vision

Three research directions

01

Investigator-initiated trials

We design and conduct investigator-initiated clinical trials that can lead to practice-changing outcomes.

02

Diverse clinical portfolio

We pursue a broad range of clinical studies across phases and tumor types that provide new treatment opportunities for patients.

03

Translational advancement

We promote translational research that bridges basic science and clinical application, defining mechanisms of response and resistance.
