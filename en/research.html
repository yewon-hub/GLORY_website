Research

Our research

Our research structure runs from questions raised in clinical practice through preclinical work to clinical trials.

Scope

The full cycle of gynecologic cancer care and research

Led by gynecologic oncologists, the GLORY Team provides comprehensive care for patients with ovarian, endometrial and cervical cancer — across diagnosis, surgery, chemotherapy and clinical trials. Standard of care, preclinical research, investigator-initiated trials and large-scale international studies form a single continuum.

This structure means questions raised at the bedside become research questions directly, and research findings return to patient care.

Standard of care

Evidence-based treatment across all stages of disease.

Preclinical research

Testing new therapeutic hypotheses in patient-derived models.

Investigator-initiated trials

Trials designed in-house from questions raised in clinical practice.

Large-scale global trials

Multinational studies conducted with global research networks.

Translational Research

Translational research topics

Our translational work centers on the tumor immune microenvironment, liquid biopsy (ctDNA) and organoid models to define mechanisms of treatment response and resistance.

ACT-K & APGOT

What is ACT-K?

ACT-K (Alliance for Clinical Trials – KGOG) is a strategic and agile research organization established to respond effectively to international clinical trials. It functions as a platform enabling KGOG to participate in and take leadership of multinational studies through global partnerships.

Mission & vision

Mission — To elevate KGOG research to global standards by enabling fast, professional and collaborative participation in international clinical trials; to secure scientific leadership and meaningful recognition for KGOG members; and to serve as a reliable, strategic partner for global sponsors and academic networks.

Vision — Global Leadership. Trusted Partnership. Korean Excellence in Trials.
